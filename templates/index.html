WoundSeg

WOUNDSEG is a deep learning–based wound segmentation research tool developed to automatically identify and delineate wound regions from clinical images.

Model Architecture

The primary segmentation model uses a U-Net architecture with an EfficientNet-B4 encoder, initialized with ImageNet-pretrained weights. The model processes wound images at a standardized resolution and produces a binary segmentation mask representing the detected wound region.

Training & Evaluation

To improve segmentation performance, the training pipeline combines Binary Cross-Entropy and Dice Loss, along with extensive image augmentation and SCSE attention in the decoder. Model performance is evaluated using Dice Score and Intersection over Union (IoU), providing quantitative measures of how closely the predicted wound region matches the reference segmentation.

Few-Shot Extension

WOUNDSEG also explores a prototype-guided few-shot segmentation approach, allowing the model to use a labelled support example to guide segmentation of a new query image. This extension investigates how wound segmentation can be adapted when only limited labelled examples are available.

The platform is intended as a research and educational tool for exploring deep learning–based wound segmentation. It is not designed to replace clinical assessment or professional medical judgment.
